	- Les "reversal setups"
	- Les trend fowlowing setups"

il est conseillé d'utiliser les stratégie "reversal setups" quand le prix est dans un "range bound market"

Description strategie #1
	- est un "reversal SETUP"
	- cette strategie est basées sur la divergenace entre higher_high/lower_low price level et lower_high/higher_low de MACD history + support and resistance level
	
	Princip:
	- la divergeance est un fort signal "reversal", cependant il n'est pas possible de trader chaque divergeance, il est nécessaire d'identifier les zones potentielles de fort effet "reversal" tel que les zones de support et de resistance
	- mecanisme ->> attendre le prix se trouve dans la zone de ces resistance et support level 
		step 01: identify S/R level on higher timeframe -> par exemple 4H
		Step 02 : Wait for MACD divergeance on lower timeframe -> par exemple 1H
		
	strategy Instruction :
		Step 1 : identify resistance and support level
		Step 2 : wait the price is inside the zone 
		Step 3 : Check for entry point
		step 3.1 : identify the lowest ( or highest ) macd histogramm point in the macd histogram divergeance zone 
		Step 3.2 : draw a horizonatline at this point 
		step 3.3 : wait the price to breake this line -> that the SELL entry point

	i share you an image about the situation to identify on Strategy1_entry.png
	This is a entry desciption for a SELL trade, the symetrical situation is to detect on a BUY trade
		
Description strategie #2
	- est un "reversal SETUP"
	- cette strategie est basées sur la divergeance entre higher_high/lower_low price level et higher_high/lower_low de MACD history + Bolliger bands
	
	SELL TRADE Princip :
	- parameters for this specific strategy 
		bollinger band length : 200
		standard deviation : 2
	- if the bollinger bands is used on a 200 EMA
	- if the price is in an strong uptrend, the price is above BB for extended period
	- after a priod above, we expect the price move back inside BB
	- in this case look for a bearish MACD divergeance 
	
	strategy Instruction :
		Step 1 : wait price for a longer period above or below BB
		Step 2 : wait for a MACD histogram and Price divergeance 
		Step 3 : wait the price close inside the BB = Entry candle
	

Description strategie #3
	- est un "trend folowing SETUP"
	- cette strategie est basées sur EMA bands + ZeroLine Pullbacks
	- for this strategy we need 4 indicators MACD + 3 EMA
		the 3 EMA are all 200 EMA from diffrent sources 
		EMA_h is calculated on the highs of the candles 
		EMA_c is calculated on the closing price of the candles 
		EMA_l is calculated on the lows of the candles 
	
	princip	
		if the price is trending this lines are providing support and resistance to the price
		in an uptrend we often see the price often pullback to the EMAs and find support
		similarly in a downtrend the price often pullback to the EMAS and find resistance
		important : the price will not always bounce exactly from the bands sometimes the price goes deeper before reversal
		combining with macd it became a strategy
		
	strategy Instruction for a buy setup
		Step 1 : the price trend is based on EMAs direction, in case of the price made a strong upmove, the price is above the bands, this is the sign the price is in an uptrend
		Step 2 : the price pullback to the EMA bands, in this case we expect the price to find support in the EMA bands area
		Step 3 : MACD fastline cross over the Signal line below the zero line ->> in an uptrend we expect the price to bounce upward after the MACD fastline touche the macd zeroline (also a sign the pullback maybe over & the price may resume the uptrend again)
		at this point we have a confluance confirmation of the MACD and the EMA bands
		Step 4 : For the Entry point, create a short term price trend line (Strategy3_entry_buy.png) and enter on the breakout of this line
	
	The symetrical behavior will act as a SELL setup

Description strategie #4
	- est un "trend folowing SETUP"
	- cette strategie est basées sur les Swap Zones + Zeroline Pullbacks
	
	principe 
		if the price cross a support zone, it can now act as a resistance zone
		if the price cross a resistance zone, it can now act as a support zone 
		this called Swapzones 
		this concept combined with MACD will be the strategy
	
	strategy instructions for BUY trade
		STEP 1 : identify the price is in a steady up  trend 
		STEP 2 : identify resistance levels 
		STEP 3 : wait the price cross this levels to became SWAP zones ->> now we have a resistance level
		STEP 4 : wait the price pullback to the the zone, in this cas we expect the price to bounce and move upward
		STEP 5 : if the MACD line is below the zero line showing signs of a support level
		STEP 6 : wait for ENTRY signal ->> check for candelstick formations (DOJI, enGOLFING, evening star, etc...) at the support level
		
	 The symetrical behavior will act as a SELL setup
