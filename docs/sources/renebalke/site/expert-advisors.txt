 Range Breakout EA 
MT5 
 One of my favourite strategies. I have been running it live for years. 
 The Range Breakout EA waits for a range to form in the morning. You pick two times of day, and the EA calculates the high and low between them. It then automatically places pending orders to trade the breakouts in either direction.

 The Fisherman EA 
MT5 
 "Fishes" for quick retracements inside major trends. 
 The Fisherman EA hunts for short counter-trend moves inside a bigger trend. Different filters set the trend direction. Before opening a trade, the EA waits for a move against the trend — and uses those retracements as the entry trigger. 
 Strategy Trend pullback 
 Markets Forex, indices 
 Timeframe M15 – H4

 ATR Candle Breakout EA 
MT5 
 Catches unusually large momentum candles — breakouts measured against the ATR. 
 The ATR Candle Breakout EA scans every closed candle on your chosen timeframe and compares its size to the Average True Range (ATR). When a candle is significantly larger than the recent average — and closes near its own extreme — the EA reads it as a genuine momentum breakout and trades in that direction. 
 Strategy ATR momentum breakout 
 Markets Forex, indices, gold 
 Timeframe H1 – H4
