About this EA 
The original idea: wait for pullbacks in the major indices, then ride the recovery move at the start of a new week. A strategy traders have used (and still use) for years. 
The EA only opens buy trades. I personally run it on index CFDs because they tend to drift higher over time. You can configure the opening and closing time and day, set TP/SL levels, and limit entries to fresh daily highs.
